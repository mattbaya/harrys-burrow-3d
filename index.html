Harry's Burrow Rescue 3D

Dig down through the 3D dirt blocks, find Harry's buried treasures, and haul them back up to his wheelbarrow. Every block you dig becomes a small dirt cube you can push out of the way or up to the surface. Harry checks each item off his Boplist — but if he wanders off, sneaky animals will steal anything left unattended! Push rocks to the surface to build walls, or drop cube-shaped poop walls to block thieves.

Controls: WASD or arrows to move/dig • I/K or PgUp/PgDn to change layer • Space to pick up/drop items & dirt cubes • Shift + direction to push objects • E for poop wall • Q for sniff radar • R to call Harry

Drag mouse to rotate camera • Scroll to zoom
